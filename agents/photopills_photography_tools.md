# PhotoPills Photography Tools

Based on the "photopills-user-guide.pdf", PhotoPills offers a variety of advanced planning tools and photography calculators. These core tools, referred to as "Pills," include:

*   **Planner:** An advanced planning tool used to plan photos involving the Sun, Moon, Milky Way, Star Trails, Meteor Showers, and Eclipses. Within the Planner's map settings, there are additional specific map tools: a Field of View tool, a Depth of Field tool, and a Sun/Moon tool (used to calculate the shooting distance needed to get a desired Sun or Moon size).
*   **Sun:** Provides daily information such as Sunrise and Sunset times, golden hour, blue hour, twilight times, and an augmented reality view of the Sun's path.
*   **Moon:** Provides Moonrise and Moonset times, Moon phase, SuperMoon dates, a Moon calendar, and an augmented reality view of the Moon's path.
*   **Exposure:** Calculates equivalent exposure times for long exposures, shooting in low light, at night, or when using ND filters.
*   **Depth of field (DoF):** Calculates the hyperfocal distance, total depth of field, and DoF near and far limits. 
*   **Field of View (FoV):** Provides horizontal, vertical, and diagonal angle of view and field of view information.
*   **DoF Table & Hyperfocal Table:** Provides depth of field and hyperfocal distance information formatted into easy-to-read tables.
*   **Night AR:** An augmented reality tool to visualize the night sky, including the Milky Way, Galactic Center, Moon, Polaris, celestial poles, and Star Trails patterns.
*   **Meteor Showers:** Offers a meteor shower calendar, peak dates/times, visibility quality, and augmented reality radiant positioning.
*   **Star Trails:** Provides Star Trails simulations and calculates the exposure time needed for a desired trail length.
*   **Spot Stars:** Calculates the maximum exposure time you can use to capture stars as bright dots without them trailing.
*   **Time lapse:** Calculates parameters like clip length, shooting intervals, total photos needed, and memory usage.
*   **Timer:** A countdown timer for long exposures, time lapses, and Star Trails.
*   **Subject distance:** Calculates the minimum shooting distance needed to fit a subject into your frame.
*   **Focal length match:** Calculates the focal length and aperture needed on a second camera (with a different sensor size) to capture the same field of view and depth of field.
